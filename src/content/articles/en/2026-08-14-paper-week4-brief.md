From Nature Photonics to ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces, PEER sweeps 12 high-impact interdisciplinary journals each week and introduces briefly only those papers touching the display industry. The reference date for inclusion is the first online publication date (2026-07-27 to 08-02). This week produced 11, short of the target of 15. Rather than padding it out, the actual survey result is reported as it stands.

> **Two candidates passed strongly this week, so there are two in-depth articles →**
> [Oxide TFT stability and contact resistance held by a laser](/en/article/2026-08-04-dgist-samsung-igzo-laser-anneal) ·
> [Sensing, memory and computation in a single flexible transistor](/en/article/2026-07-28-flexible-otft-retinomorphic)

## The other 10

### 1. Green large-scale synthesis of perovskite QDs with a low lasing threshold

*Advanced Science · 07.27 · Min Zhou, Yangzhou University · OA*

PLQY 95%, ASE threshold 50.1µJ/cm²: CsPbBr3 QDs synthesized greenly at gram scale with a bio-derived ligand work as a low-threshold laser gain material.

### 2. Structural colour from commercial liquid crystal alone, with a glass company involved

*Advanced Science · 07.30 · Saint-Gobain Recherche + Paris-Saclay and Montpellier universities · OA*

Reflective and transmissive structural colour realized by spin coating a commercial achiral liquid crystal (8CB) alone, with no chiral molecule synthesis.

### 3. A flexible organic TFT that both senses and computes

*Nature Communications · 07.27 · Tianjin University · Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications · Jilin University · OA*

Mobility 22.65cm²/V·s, photoresponse 30µs: inorganic-organic heterogeneous dielectrics integrate sensing, memory and computation in a flexible TFT. → covered as a separate in-depth article: [Sensing, memory and computation in a single flexible transistor](/en/article/2026-07-28-flexible-otft-retinomorphic)

### 4. A circularly polarized OLED from topologically chiral molecules

*Nature Communications · 07.30 · East China Normal University · Institute of Chemistry, Chinese Academy of Sciences · OA*

EQE 3.92%: the first circularly polarized OLED realized with neutral radicals on a topologically chiral [2]catenane skeleton.

### 5. A narrowband polarized OLED made by strong coupling

*Advanced Materials · 07.28 · Beijing University of Technology*

EQE 3.4%, FWHM 17.0nm: strong-coupling hybrid modes realize a narrowband, directional and polarized OLED (for near-eye displays).

### 6. A circularly polarized OLED passing 28% EQE with axially chiral molecules

*Adv. Functional Materials · 07.30 · Xi'an Jiaotong University*

EQE 28.1%: a high-efficiency circularly polarized OLED made with a pair of axially chiral MR-TADF enantiomers directly bonded at the 3,3′ positions.

### 7. Micro-LED and CMOS integration for AR displays, a review

*Laser & Photonics Rev. · 07.29 · Tae-Yeon Seong, Korea University + Taiwan and Japan*

A comprehensive comparison of the bottlenecks of each integration approach for high-resolution AR microdisplays at sub-10µm pixel pitch.

### 8. A sky-blue hyperfluorescent OLED with suppressed roll-off

*ACS Appl. Mater. Interfaces · 07.27 · Sun Yat-sen University · South China University of Technology · Wuyi University*

EQEmax 30.9%, roll-off 22.7% at 1000cd/m²: a TADF sensitizer suppresses the efficiency drop of a sky-blue OLED.

### 9. A fully solar-blind perovskite photodetector

*Laser & Photonics Rev. · 07.29 · Xiamen University · Nanjing University*

Detectivity 1.5×10¹² Jones: a solar-blind perovskite photodetector monolithically integrated on a TFT backplane, demonstrated as far as imaging.

### 10. A vacuum-deposited near-infrared perovskite LED, a record efficiency

*Advanced Materials · 08.01 · Shenzhen University · Nanjing Tech University*

EQE 16.6% (a record for vacuum-deposited NIR perovskite LEDs): a zwitterionic interlayer makes a near-infrared LED compatible with existing OLED deposition infrastructure.

**Selection criteria**: all papers newly published in the 12 major journals on our registry with a first online publication date between 2026-07-27 and 08-02 and directly related to the display industry (panels, materials, processes, equipment). The text is based on the published abstracts and bibliographic information.
