From Nature Photonics to ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces, PEER sweeps 12 high-impact interdisciplinary journals each week and introduces briefly only those papers touching the display industry. The reference date for inclusion is the first online publication date (2026-07-20 to 07-26).

> **This week's lead paper is covered in depth →** [Mobility and stability of an oxide TFT, both held by fluorine](/en/article/2026-07-27-yonsei-igzo-tft-implantation)

## The other 21

### 1. An OLED integrating memory and computation for 3D displays

*Nature Photonics · 07.21 · Lixuan Liu, Fuzhou University*

A concept integrating memory, computation and emission in a single OLED to reconstruct a NeRF-based 3D display.

### 2. A spin LED tuned by exciton interactions

*Nature Photonics · 07.23 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

Spin dynamics controlled through exciton-exciton interactions to realize a high-luminance spin LED.

### 3. A single OLED that learns and shows by itself

*Advanced Materials · 07.23 · Huipeng Chen, Fuzhou University*

Display fidelity 90.86%: a single OLED visualizes its own neuromorphic computation results.

### 4. A full-colour reflective display working across all temperatures

*Advanced Materials · 07.25 · Kaiyue Zhang, CAS*

Colour switching speed 267nm/s, operating from -20 to 45℃: a full-colour reflective display realized with electrophoretic colloidal crystals.

### 5. A skin-touch display that lights up under pressure

*Advanced Materials · 07.22 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

A skin-mimicking electroluminescent tactile display mapping pressure and emission quantitatively.

### 6. 30nm pixels drawn with metal-ligand coordination

*Advanced Materials · 07.22 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

Resolution below 30nm, a full-colour pattern of 39,000 pixels: a direct lithography resist for metal halides was developed.

### 7. An ultralow-threshold nanolaser running at room temperature

*Advanced Materials · 07.22 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

Threshold 0.03 W/cm²: a room-temperature WS2 nanolaser realized by twist engineering of a photonic crystal cavity.

### 8. An upconversion laser emitting at multiple wavelengths

*Advanced Materials · 07.23 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

Threshold 140nW: a multi-wavelength upconversion laser based on lanthanide-doped coordination polymer glass.

### 9. Micro-LED mass transfer at 99.8% yield

*Adv. Functional Materials · 07.23 · Young Rag Do, Kookmin University · Samsung Electronics*

Assembly yield 99.8%, transfer yield 100%, placement error 1.62µm: dielectrophoretic assembly even repairs defective micro-LED subpixels.

### 10. A bicolour phosphor film for laser lighting

*Laser & Photonics Rev. · 07.21 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

Thermal conductivity 74.3 W/m·K, luminous efficacy 247.5 lm/W: a high-brightness phosphor-in-glass film for laser lighting.

### 11. Quantum yield of 98% without lead

*Laser & Photonics Rev. · 07.21 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

Co-occupying the Sb/In sites raises the quantum yield of a lead-free halide phosphor to a record.

### 12. A blue GaN laser, a record 3.2W output

*Laser & Photonics Rev. · 07.23 · Nanjing University · CAS · Suzhou Ganbright*

A photonic-crystal surface-emitting laser giving 3.2W output in blue at 430.5nm. The highest on record.

### 13. 10 years of efficiency gains in 2D light emitters, a review

*Laser & Photonics Rev. · 07.22 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

A summary of how the quantum efficiency of transition metal dichalcogenide light emitters rose from 0.001% to 10% over 10 years.

### 14. Blue OLED and radioluminescence from one emitter

*Laser & Photonics Rev. · 07.21 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

EQE 7.07%: a single hot-exciton emitter realizes efficient blue electroluminescence and ultrafast radioluminescence together.

### 15. High-throughput production of perovskite QD films

*ACS Appl. Mater. Interfaces · 07.25 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

Colour gamut NTSC 126%: a process producing perovskite QD films at high throughput through modular precursor feeding.

### 16. A transparent silver nanowire film that shields electromagnetic waves

*ACS Appl. Mater. Interfaces · 07.25 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

Sheet resistance 16.9Ω/sq at 81.6% transmittance: a photonically sintered Cu@AgNW film capturing both EMI shielding and heat dissipation.

### 17. A reflective display textile woven from liquid crystal fibres

*Small · 07.21 · Peining Chen, Fudan University*

Contrast ratio 2.09 (at 10,000lux): a reflective textile display woven from cholesteric liquid crystal fibres, visible even in bright places.

### 18. Circularly polarized emitting clusters tuned in colour by ligands

*Small · 07.21 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

PLQY near 100%: aliphatic ligands tune the Cu-Cu distance to realize colour-tunable circularly polarized luminescence.

### 19. Circularly polarized perovskite nanowires made with chiral ligands

*Small · 07.24 · author affiliation to be confirmed*

Chiral ligand synthesis produces circularly polarized luminescent perovskite nanowires tunable across the visible.

### 20. Designing the anode and cathode interfaces of a QLED at once

*ACS Appl. Mater. Interfaces · 07.23 · Umberto Giovanella, CNR-SCITEC · OA*

Turn-on voltage 1.7V: a fully solution-processed device designing the anode and cathode interfaces of a QLED together, using only commercial polymers.

### 21. A circularly polarized LED made from chiral carbon dots

*Nature Communications · 07.22 · Yuxin Shi et al. · OA*

Supramolecular self-assembly of fluorinated carbon dots induces chirality to realize a bright circularly polarized emitting LED (original not confirmed, needs review).
